# Advanced study modules

## Original-language study

`#/linguistics/GEN/1?verse=1&edition=eng-web` opens two pinned historical dictionaries: 5,624 Greek records and 8,674 Hebrew/Aramaic records. Records without headwords stay explicit. Counts come from `data/linguistics/manifest.json`; dictionary entries are not Bible editions.

The importer (`python3 scripts/import-lexicon.py --verify`) independently parses the public-domain XML at [Open Scriptures revision 0acd2f2](https://github.com/openscriptures/strongs/tree/0acd2f251c2d35ff8db2dece4e0593979d3ac223). The complete publisher notices and source/data checksums are retained. It does not use separately licensed JSON derivatives. An explicit download verifies both dictionaries and complete notices for offline lookup; cancellation retains prior complete packs and scoped removal preserves private experiments. Search supports identifiers, source script, transliteration and historical definitions; normalized matching preserves displayed diacritics.

Private translation exercises bind an author-selected dictionary entry to an exact installed Bible passage and source revision. Two wording choices and notes can be saved, restored, exported and deleted. These choices are private user writing. No verse-level alignment, interlinear, morphology tagging or reviewer-approved translation alternatives are implied. An historical dictionary is not evidence that one English gloss fits every occurrence.

## Athens pilot

`#/time-travel/ACT/17?verse=16&edition=eng-web` provides four reading stops, two evidence/time frames, three perspective prompts, exact selected-edition Scripture, optional software 3D, local device narration when an eligible voice exists, and a complete standalone 2D HTML study pack. Every pack checks display, document export, redistribution and offline permissions against one source revision. The complete publisher notice is verified against the current build inventory and embedded unchanged; a missing or altered notice prevents export.

The map and scene are original schematic geometry, not surveyed archaeology or a reconstruction of Paul’s route. Notes are AI-assisted research drafts with human review pending. No invented character dialogue, external models or photographs are bundled.

Research checked on 5 November 2026:

- [ASCSA excavation project](https://www.ascsa.org/excavations/athenian-agora): civic setting and excavation beginning in 1931.
- [ASCSA excavation account](https://www.ascsa.org/excavations/athenian-agora/about-the-excavations): public documents, measurement standards and jurors’ evidence.
- [USCCB Acts 17:19 note](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/acts/17): Areopagus can identify the hill or council; the exact speech venue is not established by this pilot.
- [Greek Ministry of Culture](https://www.culture.gov.gr/en/service/SitePages/view.aspx?iiD=1516): 1997–2004 conservation and presentation work. Modern visitor routes do not establish the historical episode’s location.

Accessible 2D reading stays available when the optional scene cannot be drawn. Keyboard and button controls move the scene; it never auto-animates. Narration is optional, checks source permissions, and offers only browser-reported local voices in the exact source language. Local voice reporting does not establish independently measured offline behavior on every device.

## Verification and remaining boundaries

Unit checks verify imported lexical counts/checksums, immutable source identity, invalid and conflicting restores, denied permissions, missing passages, source changes during exports, cancellation, safe HTML output and bounded scene geometry. Browser cases exercise source lookup, private persistence, the integrated blind-reveal flow, tour navigation, 320 px reflow and actual downloads. Current release receipts and unrun checks belong in `docs/agent/PROJECT_STATUS.md`.

Nearby transport and blind-interpretation details are maintained in their module documents. Real independent human editorial review, modern linguistic alignment, licensed recorded narration and physical two-device offline verification remain separate acceptance gates.
