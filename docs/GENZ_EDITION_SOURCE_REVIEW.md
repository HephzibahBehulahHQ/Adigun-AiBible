# Gen Z edition source and rights review

**Decision: no external Gen Z source is eligible for import.** The project-owner request is to represent Gen Z as a Bible edition in its own right. The repository has no complete standalone Gen Z corpus: the installed edition catalog contains no Gen Z edition, the `genz` preset resolves to the World English Bible, and PR #40 supplied five Genesis 1:1–5 entries as reviewed paraphrases. PR #70 removed the visible paraphrase and pending labels, but did not create a full edition corpus.

## Official sources reviewed

- [Bible for Gen Z](https://bibleforgenz.com/) says its Gen Z wording is a paraphrase of the World English Bible, shown as a reading aid beside the original rather than as Scripture on its own. It does not meet the project's requirement for a distinct edition.
- [Angely: Gen Z Bible terms](https://biblegenz.com/terms) identifies its Gen Z texts as original works owned by Infinitas Studio. Its use license prohibits copying, modifying, public display, commercial use, and mirroring. No redistribution grant suitable for this project is provided.
- [The Gen Z Bible about page](https://www.genz-bible.com/about) describes that product as a paraphrase and recommends reading it alongside established translations. No license to redistribute its text was identified.
- [API.Bible's current version catalog](https://www.api.bible/bibles) distinguishes open-access texts from copyright-protected Bibles that require standard or unique licenses. Its published catalog has no "Gen Z" match. API.Bible is a licensing/access catalog, not a source that grants rights to an unlisted text.

These sources do not provide a complete Gen Z Bible edition that is both consistent with the owner's definition and cleared for redistribution. No third-party text was copied.

## Project action

Keep the existing reader-label correction from PR #70. Do not advertise the WEB-plus-editorial overlay as a standalone Gen Z Bible, and do not fill missing books or verses with generated text. A complete edition requires an authoritative complete text and a redistribution grant covering this project's public deployment.
