# Adigun Edition authoring

Adigun is creating an original English Bible edition under the owner's direction. It is its own version, with its own text and identity. It is not sourced from a third-party Gen Z product.

## Source basis

The edition is authored from the project's pinned World English Bible text (`eng-web`). The WEB publisher dedicates the text to the public domain and expressly permits adaptation and redistribution, asking that changed text not use the WEB name: [official WEB notice](https://ebible.org/engwebp/copyright.htm). The edition identity is **Adigun Edition** (`adigun-edition`).

The edition preserves the project's WEB 66-book order and every source chapter/verse address. Its voice should be unmistakably conversational and current: natural youth idioms, direct speech, varied cadence, and occasional playful turns or emojis where they fit the passage. Avoid flattening it into standard modern English or sprinkling slang mechanically into every sentence. Keep the meaning, people, actions, and relationships faithful to the source; do not add events, doctrine, or claims. Use the supplied Genesis and Joshua examples as tone references, not text to copy. The owner approved Genesis 1:1–5 as the project's current voice reference.

## Authoring state

- Complete chapter candidates cover Genesis 1–24, all 659 verses, under `content/genz/ot/GEN/`; each chapter has its own numbered JSON file.
- Authoring scope is the complete 66-book manifest, followed book by book in canonical order. Genesis must be complete before Exodus work begins.
- Primary Codex owns the Old Testament from Genesis through Micah. The assigned second agent checks Malachi first and then works backward through Zechariah, Haggai, Zephaniah, Habakkuk, and Nahum; both lanes meet without overlap.
- The New Testament candidate corpus is reported complete and must be checked against the source manifest before the project claims full-canon completion.
- Reader integration publishes only completed chapters in an explicitly scoped portion manifest. The reader never substitutes WEB wording for missing Adigun text or presents an editorial overlay as the Adigun Edition.

No third-party edition text is used. The reader name is **Adigun Edition**.
