## Edition counts and acceptance

Only a distinct authentic installed text edition that actually opens increases the reader count. Additional editions are measured beyond the three-edition baseline. Keep separate full Bible, New Testament, portions, historical, audio, metadata-only and awaiting-rights totals. Duplicate provider IDs, themes, UI locales, voices and paraphrase registers never inflate counts.

Every new slice needs source/rights and review evidence, preservation of existing personal data, material-risk checks and verification on the deployed revision. Test fixtures cannot satisfy production content review, real service authorization or two-device transport gates.

## Remaining implementation versus external gates

Unbuilt software includes full linguistic/alignment study tools, speaker audience/quotation-chain features, genealogy/relationship atlas, Nigerian adaptation renderer, multiplayer detective state, and complete locale dictionaries/book aliases including Hausa and Igbo. Broader historical imports, canon profiles and researched context are content engineering, followed by independent review.

The acquisition inventory contains 110 provider records labelled Public Domain. Those labels are research leads, not verified rights grants, but they are not all blocked by commercial licensing. Exact notices, revisions, distinctness, coverage, storage/performance and reproducible imports must be checked before extending the 29 Bible editions in main. The 100+ additional-edition target remains incomplete.

Independent human editorial/native-language review, restricted edition licenses, dedicated backend/provider activation, permitted recorded narration and physical two-device offline testing remain genuine external gates. The latest Law Diary addendum puts true OS widgets outside scope; the web lock-screen card is implemented and needs no native-platform blocker.
